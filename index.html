The evening wrap: tap End my day, check the totals, then Send to Command Center — the Activity row and the full log write straight into the sheet.


Dialling: tap a number to call. Text and Email open with the message already written. Press and hold the number to copy it.


Statuses: the blue row is what you did (LM, DLM for a call with no message, TX, EM, BAD# for a dead number). The traffic-light row is what came of it. LM and DLM count up, so each card tells you how many times you have tried.


Where this comes from: the people and their numbers are read live from the Command Center pipelines — edit a row and the deck has it. What to say on the call comes from the project. Nothing here is baked into the app.
